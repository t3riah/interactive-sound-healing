01 — Foundations

The core principles

Five ideas explain almost everything in sound healing. Master these and the rest — bowls, tuning forks, chanting, gongs — makes sense.

1. Vibration

All sound is vibration travelling as pressure waves through air, water and tissue. Pitch is how fast it vibrates — measured in Hertz (cycles per second). The body is ~60% water, an excellent sound conductor, which is why you feel bass in your chest.

2. Resonance

Every object has natural frequencies at which it vibrates most easily. A tuning fork can make another identical fork sing without touch. Sound healing aims tones at the body hoping for sympathetic resonance — like pushing a swing at its own rhythm.

3. Entrainment

Rhythmic systems sync up. Pendulum clocks on one wall align; heart rate, breath and brainwaves can drift toward a steady external rhythm. Slow, regular sound tends to invite slower breathing — the basis of most relaxation practice.

4. Harmonics

Real sounds are never one frequency. A voice or bowl produces a fundamental plus overtones at 2×, 3×, 4× that frequency. That overtone recipe — the timbre — is why a flute and a violin on the same note sound utterly different.

5. Intention & Listening

Attention changes experience. Focused listening lowers the threshold for noticing subtle sensation and shifts autonomic state. Practitioners pair tone with intention; researchers would call part of this expectation, ritual and deep rest — all still real effects.

What is a Hertz?

1 Hz = 1 vibration per second.
A4 = 440 Hz means the air pulses 440 times a second. Double it → 880 Hz, one octave higher, same note name. Halve it → 220 Hz, one octave lower. Octaves are the universe’s 2:1 ratio.

03 — Waveforms & Timbre

Same pitch, different shape, different feeling

A waveform is the shape of vibration over time. Shape = harmonics = character. Tap any card to hear it at A4 (440 Hz).

Harmonics, in plain terms

A sine wave has one frequency — laboratory-pure, like a tuning fork. Every natural sound adds overtones:

Fundamental 100 Hz
+ 200 Hz (octave) + 300 Hz (octave + fifth / 12th) + 400 Hz…

Triangle keeps only odd harmonics, softly. Square keeps odd harmonics, strongly — clarinet-like. Sawtooth keeps all harmonics — strings, brass, buzz. Singing bowls are prized because their inharmonic partials shimmer and beat against each other.

Amplitude, frequency, phase

Frequency → pitch (Hz). Amplitude → loudness (decibels; +10 dB ≈ twice as loud perceived). Phase → where in its cycle a wave starts — inaudible alone, decisive when waves combine: in-phase waves amplify, opposite-phase waves cancel (the principle behind noise-cancelling headphones).

ADSR — Attack, Decay, Sustain, Release — describes how a sound’s amplitude unfolds. A gong’s long release is its therapy: no sharp edges for the nervous system to brace against.

04 — Binaural Beats

Two tones in, one beat your brain creates

Play 200 Hz in the left ear and 210 Hz in the right. Neither ear hears a pulse — but the brainstem, comparing the two, perceives a third, phantom 10 Hz beat. That difference tone is the binaural beat.

Headphones are essential — speakers mix the tones in air and you just hear ordinary interference beats instead.

Evidence note: studies find small, inconsistent effects on anxiety, attention and sleep. Treat binaural beats as a listening practice with plausible entrainment mechanisms — not a medical treatment.

Brainwave bands — pick a state

Brainwaves are electrical rhythms measured in the same unit — Hz. The entrainment hypothesis: a beat in a band may nudge brain activity toward that band. Delta for sleep, Alpha for calm focus, Gamma for binding perception.

05 — Healing Frequencies

The Solfeggio frequencies & key tones

A separate modern tradition: these nine specific frequencies borrow their Ut–Re–Mi names from the medieval chant scale, but they are not the notes of the C-major scale in Section 06 (where Mi = E, 329.63 Hz). Here Mi = 528 Hz by this tradition's own assignment. The associations below are the traditional ones taught in sound healing — tap any tone to hear it as a pure sine.

432 Hz vs 440 Hz

Concert pitch A4=440 Hz was standardised in the 20th century. 432 Hz is ~1.8% flatter — many listeners describe it as warmer. No robust evidence favours either medically; the audible difference itself is a fine listening exercise. Both are in the Frequency Lab quick row above.

Schumann Resonance — 7.83 Hz

The Earth–ionosphere cavity resonates at ~7.83 Hz, near the Theta/Alpha border. Too low to hear as pitch (infrasound), it is often cited in healing lore as “Earth’s heartbeat” and reproduced as a binaural or pulse rate rather than a tone.

136.10 Hz — the “Om” tone

Calculated by Hans Cousto in The Cosmic Octave by transposing Earth’s orbital period up many octaves. Tuning forks at 136.1 Hz are widely used in practice and correspond roughly to C♯. A beautiful example of mapping astronomy → sound via octaves.

07 — Cymatics

Sound made visible: geometry

Sprinkle sand on a vibrating plate (a Chladni plate) and it flees the moving areas, collecting in still lines — drawing the sound’s geometry. Higher frequencies make more intricate patterns. Choose a pattern below: this canvas simulates the nodal geometry.

Chladni figures

Ernst Chladni (1787) bowed metal plates covered in sand. Each resonant mode draws a unique mandala. The pattern is the frequency, frozen in space.

Water & Faraday waves

Tone through water creates standing-wave fountains and lattices — the basis of modern cymatics photography used in installations and album art.

Lissajous figures

Feed one tone to the X-axis and another to the Y-axis of an oscilloscope: simple ratios (1:1, 2:1, 3:2) draw clean loops. Consonant intervals literally look harmonious.

08 — Every Way to Capture Sound

Sound, translated into everything else

A sound is information. It can be perfectly captured, stored, and re-expressed in many forms — each reveals something the ear alone misses.

〰️

Waveform

Time domain. Amplitude vs time — the oscilloscope / DAW view. Shows loudness, envelope, transients. Loses nothing: play it back and you hear the original.

🌈

Spectrogram

Time × frequency × intensity as colour. Reveals harmonics, vibrato and formants invisible in a waveform. How speech recognition “sees” sound.

🎼

Notation

Symbols on a staff. Pitch = vertical position, duration = note shape. A recipe for re-performance — lossy for timbre, perfect for melody and harmony.

🎨

Colour

Chromesthesia mapping. 12 pitch classes → 12 hues. Via octave transposition, sound frequencies mathematically continue into light frequencies.

✦

Geometry

Cymatics & Lissajous. Standing waves draw mandalas; interval ratios draw loops. Consonance becomes visible symmetry.

🔢

Number & Ratio

Hz, ratios, cents. Octave 2:1, fifth 3:2, major third 5:4. Pythagoras built a cosmos from these. Every tuning system is arithmetic made audible.

💾

Digital Data

Samples & MIDI. Audio = 44,100 amplitude measurements/sec (CD). MIDI stores note, velocity, timing — instructions, not sound. Both perfectly reproducible.

✋

Touch & Body

Vibration & bone conduction. Low frequencies are felt, not heard — vibroacoustic chairs, deaf percussionists performing barefoot. Sound as tactile language.

💡

Light

Transposed octaves & visualisers. Multiply Hz by 2⁴⁰ ≈ light. In performance: colour organs, synaesthetic visuals, laser Lissajous — sound driving light in real time.

🏛️

Architecture

Space as instrument. Cathedral reverb, whispering galleries, Helmholtz resonators built into walls. Buildings store and shape sound — the oldest sound technology.

🧠

Brainwaves

Neural entrainment. Rhythmic sound → measurable EEG rhythms. The binaural beat exists only in this translation: two sounds become one brain rhythm.

🌿

Nature’s Notation

Birdsong sonograms, whale calls. Scientists transcribe animal sound into spectrograms to read dialects — proof the mapping works across species.

09 — Practice

Instruments of the practice

Tibetan / Himalayan bowls — metal, struck or rim-sung; rich inharmonic partials and slow beats between them. Placed on or near the body in “sound massage.”
Crystal singing bowls — quartz, usually tuned to specific notes/chakras; very pure, loud, long sustain. Handle volume with care.
Tuning forks — precise single frequencies (e.g. 128 Hz weighted forks for body contact, 528 Hz unweighted for listening).
Gongs, chimes, drums, voice — gongs for broadband “sound baths,” frame drums for rhythm/entrainment, and overtone chanting — one voice producing two audible notes at once.
Safety: keep listening levels moderate (under ~85 dB for extended listening), use extra care with crystal bowls near ears, and treat sound healing as a complement to — never a replacement for — medical care.
Questions

Essential knowledge, answered

Is sound healing scientifically proven?

Parts are well-grounded: music and slow sound reliably reduce perceived stress and can lower heart rate and blood pressure modestly; vibration and entrainment are real physics. Specific claims — that 528 Hz repairs DNA, or a frequency cures an organ — are traditional beliefs without robust clinical evidence. Honest practice holds both.

What frequency should I start with?

For grounding and body-feel, 128–174 Hz. For calm focus, Alpha binaural beats (8–12 Hz difference on a ~200 Hz carrier). For an uplifting listen, 528 Hz. Let preference lead — the “best” frequency is the one you will actually sit with.

Why do octaves sound like the same note?

Because every harmonic of the higher note coincides with a harmonic of the lower. The brain reads that perfect overlap as identity of pitch class — the foundation of all scales, colour mapping and the Cosmic Octave.

Can I do this without instruments?

Yes — humming is the oldest tool. Hum on a slow exhale and feel the vibration in your chest and skull (try the seed sounds: LAM, VAM, RAM, YAM, HAM, OM). This page’s labs are also instruments: any tone here can anchor a 5-minute listening sit.

A simple 10-minute session

1) 2 min silence, notice breath. 2) Play 174 Hz in the Lab, sine wave, low volume — 3 min. 3) Switch to an Alpha binaural beat with headphones — 3 min. 4) Play your chakra note on the piano and hum along — 2 min. End in silence and notice what changed.
